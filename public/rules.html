TOURNAMENT REGULATIONS

All participants are required to adhere to these rules.

01

MATCH PROCEDURES

  • All matches must be played at the scheduled time. Teams have a 15-minute grace period to join the lobby.
  • Failure to field a full roster (5 players) after the grace period will result in a forfeit.
  • The Match Host (Team A) is responsible for creating the custom lobby and communicating the Riot ID via the dashboard.
  • Server selection must be agreed upon by both teams. Default is Singapore 1.
02

VETO PROCESS

  • The map veto must be conducted entirely through the Tournament Dashboard.
  • Teams have 45 seconds per turn during the veto phase.
  • Failure to make a selection within the time limit will result in a randomized pick/ban.
  • Teams are permitted two (2) tactical pauses during the veto phase, each lasting up to 2 minutes.
03

IN-GAME RULES

  • Pauses: Each team is allowed two (2) tactical pauses per map. Pauses must be called during the buy phase.
  • Technical pauses must be communicated immediately to an admin via the lobby chat or Discord.
  • Exploits, bugs, or third-party software are strictly prohibited and will result in an immediate ban.
04

SCORE REPORTING

  • The winning team is required to submit the final score and a screenshot of the post-match scoreboard via the dashboard.
  • The opposing team must verify the score. If a score is falsified, use the Dispute button.
  • Submitting falsified evidence will result in disqualification.
